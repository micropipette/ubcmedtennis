Who we are

More than just tennis.

Medical school is demanding. We believe physical activity and real social connection are the best antidotes. Whether you've never held a racket or you want to keep a competitive edge, there's a court for you here.

Events range from casual rallies to structured round-robins, with plenty of chances to meet students across all years and across our distributed sites in British Columbia. Pickleball is now officially part of the club.

Giving back

Rec club first, community always.
